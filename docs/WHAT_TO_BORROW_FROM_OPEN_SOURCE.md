# What to borrow from open source

## PriceGhost

Borrow the ideas of price history, multiple extraction strategies, price confirmation and alerting.

Do not copy the whole product as the foundation because it is too e-commerce-specific.

## changedetection.io

Borrow page diff, restock alerts and change filtering ideas.

Do not use it as the core product database because it is not built around canonical items and supplier comparison.

## Crawlee

Use Crawlee as the crawler runtime: retries, request handling, Cheerio/Playwright support and queue patterns.

## Crawl4AI

Use later for complex pages, medical price lists, tables and schema-based extraction.

## n8n

Use for quick prototypes, Telegram alerts and simple MVP workflows.
